Post-pilot checks, fixed after results/20261004T142130Z and before this run.
These are explicit follow-ups, not retroactively preregistered pilot endpoints.

1. Stronger one-setting baselines, every one of the ten circuits:
all-X and all-Y as well as all-Z, the product basis maximizing the mean
feature variance on the same three design points, and the selected basis
with identity weights. Same weight<=2 bank; validate on the same held-out
points for paired comparison. Variance selection and rank selection each
inspect the same full basis bank. This exposes whether merely changing the
default Z basis already gives full rank.

2. Finite-shot comparison adds fixed anisotropic diagonal, first random
diagonal/dense candidates, and selected diagonal, to the initial identity
and selected-dense comparison. The source circuit, perturbation direction,
input pairs, and fixed contrast remain exactly those of the pilot. All
methods reuse identical joint outcome samples. This is a local, matched
contrast diagnostic chosen from the selected metric, not a task-level
performance comparison, test power or neutral optimal contrast for each arm.

3. Exact certificates verify all ten one-basis designs and all ten fixed
diagonal local-XYZ designs after discovery. Two modular nonzero minors plus
the source K upper bound give generic rank K. No successful subset is chosen.

4. After the stronger baselines were inspected, test a mechanistic control:
Hubregtsen 4q1L, add six Z_q Z_r correlations to four local Z features.
They are available from the same ZZZZ setting if joint bit strings are kept.
Prove rank <=4 from absence of the last four symbols in exact feature
expansions, and seek a matching modular minor. This is a post-hoc explanatory
example, not an additional independently chosen benchmark family.
