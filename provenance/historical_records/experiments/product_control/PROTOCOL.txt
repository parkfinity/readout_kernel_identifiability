its diagonal entangling tail. Under this assumption both estimators are
unbiased, and products of sample means have exact covariance obtained
from independent one-qubit sample means. Check the assumption directly
against dense-state probabilities at every tested displaced state.
Compare exact contrast variances, Monte Carlo variance calibration, and
paired error. Verify direct-parity samples against the existing Linux v2
archive. Report ratios by panel, not thousands of independent discoveries.

This is a additional, local follow-up, not a preregistered experiment,
new hardware run, or independent server reproduction of the product arm.
